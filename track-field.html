The 2027 season

First day of practice is Monday, February 8, 2027.

During the first week, we’ll be conducting tryouts from Monday to Thursday. Every athlete is required to attend unless you’re still competing in a winter sport, in which case you will be given the opportunity to try out once your season is completed.

You must be cleared before you can practice

Athletic clearance has to be complete before the first day of practice. That means Sunday, February 7 at the latest — and since nobody is processing clearances on a Sunday, the real deadline is Friday, February 5.

There are no exceptions. If you miss tryouts because you are not cleared, you will not be given a makeup tryout, which could drastically affect your event group placement.

What to expect at tryouts

During the first four days, we will evaluate athletes across a range of events, from short sprints and hurdles to jumps and throws. At the end of the four-day tryout cycle, we will place athletes in event areas where we have determined they can have the greatest impact on our team.
