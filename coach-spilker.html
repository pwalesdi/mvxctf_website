Logan Spilker

Assistant coach, throws

Logan Spilker is in his 8th year coaching at Monte Vista. He began coaching immediately after high school for two years before rejoining the staff in 2021. As a Monte Vista alumnus, Logan competed in the shot put and discus and was ranked 26th in the nation for Olympic weightlifting during his senior year.

After high school, he continued his athletic career at Diablo Valley College, competing in the hammer throw and qualifying for the NorCal Championships in back-to-back years.

During his time coaching at Monte Vista, Logan has helped athletes achieve nine top-10 all-time marks in school history. Notably, Ash Rosati broke one of the school's oldest shot put records under his guidance.
