The program

Program record

Monte Vista cross country has won 14 North Coast Section team championships, and six different Mustangs have won an NCS individual title — more than any other school in the section.

Cross country 14 NCS team titles Cross country, all-time. Girls 12, boys 2.
Cross country 3 NCS titles in four years Girls Division 2 cross country champions in 2022, 2024 and 2025.
Cross country and track & field 21 Section titles since 2019 Individual and relay championships on the track and the course.
Cross country and track & field 77 All-NCS honors Earned by 31 different athletes since 2019. Top 6 at the Meet of Champions or top 7 at NCS D2
  • 2025 · Cross country Three of the top six finishers at NCS Division 2 were Monte Vista sophomores — Kaylie O'Connell first, Nadya Makarova fifth, Ava Padilla sixth.
  • All-time · Cross country Six different Mustangs have won an NCS individual title, more than any other school in the section. Alexandra Powell and Kaylie O'Connell took the last two.
  • 2022–2024 · Track & field Three consecutive EBAL girls championships, and NCS Meet of Champions runners-up in 2023.
  • 2019–2025 · Cross country Fifteen girls top-10 finishes at NCS Division 2 cross country, the most of any school in the division.
