Coaches

Dan Graham

Assistant coach, pole vault

Coach Dan Graham enters his 40th year of coaching and has led the pole vault program at Monte Vista High School (2001–present). He previously coached at Mt. Eden High School (1987–2000) and has developed numerous top-level athletes. Over his career, he has coached multiple school record holders and 18 of the top 20 performances in school history. His expertise in pole vault continues to drive success, reinforcing a tradition of excellence in the event.
