Brittany Williams

Assistant coach, sprints and relays

Coach Brittany Williams, a Monte Vista alumna and former school record holder in the 100m, returns for her 4th season as an assistant coach specializing in sprints and relays. She competed collegiately at Chico State University, bringing valuable experience to the program. In her first year of coaching, she led the sprint squad to 11 top-10 all-time performances and helped guide the 4x100m relay team to a new school record. As both an athlete and coach, Coach Williams has played a key role in shaping Monte Vista's sprint program and continues to build on its success.
