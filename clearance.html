Athletic clearance

Clear through Home Campus

  1. Create or sign in to your Home Campus account.
  2. Select Monte Vista High School and the cross country or track & field season.
  3. Upload a current physical and complete every required form.
  4. Wait for the green “cleared” status before day one.
