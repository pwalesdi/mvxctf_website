The program

Program record

Monte Vista distance has won 14 North Coast Section team championships, and has put more different individual section cross country champions on the podium than any other school in the NCS.

14 NCS team titles Cross country, all-time. Girls 12, boys 2.
3 NCS titles in four years Girls Division 2 cross country champions in 2022, 2024 and 2025.
21 Section titles since 2019 Individual and relay championships on the track and the course.
77 All-NCS honors Earned by 31 different athletes since 2019. Top 6 at the Meet of Champions or top 7 at NCS D2
  • 2025 · Cross country Three of the top six finishers at NCS Division 2 were Monte Vista sophomores — Kaylie O'Connell first, Nadya Makarova fifth, Ava Padilla sixth.
  • All-time · Cross country Six different Mustangs have won an NCS individual title, more than any other school in the section. Alexandra Powell and Kaylie O'Connell took the last two.
  • 2022–2024 · Track & field Three consecutive EBAL girls championships, and NCS Meet of Champions runners-up in 2023.
  • 2019–2025 · Depth Fifteen girls top-10 finishes at NCS Division 2 cross country, the most of any school in the division.

Before the first practice

Clearance process

Three things, in this order. Step 1 has to be finished before an athlete can set foot on the course.

1

Clear through Home Campus

  1. Create or sign in to your Home Campus account.
  2. Select Monte Vista High School and the cross country or track & field season.
  3. Upload a current physical and complete every required form.
  4. Wait for the green “cleared” status before day one.
Start clearance Step-by-step guide (PDF)
2

Team donation — $600

Check is preferred, payable to Monte Vista Cross Country. Card payments go through the MVHS webstore. No cash, Venmo or Zelle. Many employers match.

Pay on the MVHS webstore Donation & corporate matching
3

Show up to practice

Practice runs six days a week at Monte Vista. Start times vary by day — check the schedule below before the first week.
